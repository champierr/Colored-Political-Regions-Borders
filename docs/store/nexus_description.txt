[center][size=6][b]Colored Political Regions & Borders[/b][/size]
[size=4]A political map for Mount & Blade: Warband[/size][/center]

Every town, castle and village region on the world map takes the colour of the kingdom that owns it. Kingdoms are separated by a dark border line with a soft glow in each side's colour. Zoom out and you see a political map; zoom in and you see the normal terrain, with the borders still there.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Zoom out:[/b] the map shows the kingdoms in their own colours (the same colours as the town names on the map).
[*][b]Zoom in:[/b] the colour fades away and you see the normal terrain. The borders stay.
[*][b]Always up to date:[/b] when a castle or town is captured, its region and villages change colour right away.
[*][b]Ctrl + 1:[/b] opens a small panel where you set how strong the colours are (0-100 %). Let go of the bar and the map shows it; Done saves it with your game, Esc or Cancel goes back.
[*][b]Ctrl + 2:[/b] pick your own kingdom's colour from a colour field. Let go of the mouse and your land, borders and town names show it; Done saves it with your game, Esc or Cancel brings the old colour back. Needs a kingdom of your own (a town, castle or village you hold for yourself).
[*]Sea and rivers are not coloured.
[*]Works with [b]Native[/b] and with other mods through the [b]CPR&B Map Patcher[/b] program.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Which file do I download?[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]CPRB-Map-Patcher[/b] (recommended): a small program that adds the map to the mod you choose, [b]Native included[/b]. One click to install, one click to remove.
[*][b]Native module[/b] (optional): a ready mod folder (Native with the map inside). Use it if you don't want to change your original Native.
[*][b]Install video[/b] (optional): a one minute video showing every step.
[/list]
Every download has a [b]HOW_TO_INSTALL.txt[/b] file inside with the same steps.

[size=5][b]How to install (patcher)[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Download [b]CPRB-Map-Patcher[/b] from the Files tab and open the zip. Inside are [i]CPRB-Map-Patcher.exe[/i] and [i]HOW_TO_INSTALL.txt[/i].
[*]Double-click [i]CPRB-Map-Patcher.exe[/i].
Windows may show a blue window: [i]"Windows protected your PC"[/i]. This happens with small programs that are not signed by a company. Click [i]More info[/i], then [i]Run anyway[/i].
[*]At the top of the window, click the list and choose your mod (for example [i]Native[/i]). Steam and Steam Workshop mods are listed automatically.
Not in the list? Click [i]Browse...[/i] and choose the mod's folder (the one with a [i]module.ini[/i] file inside). To find it: Steam > Library > right-click Mount & Blade: Warband > Manage > Browse local files > Modules.
[*]Optional: set the colour strength with the slider (60 % is a good start).
[*]Click [b]Install / Update[/b] and wait for the line [i]"Installed CPR&B ... into ..."[/i]. Close the window.
[*]Start Warband. In the start window (launcher), choose the same mod and click [i]Play[/i]. Start a [b]new game[/b].
[*]On the world map, press [b]Space[/b] if the game is paused. The colours appear as soon as the game runs.
[/list]
[b]Uninstall:[/b] open the patcher, choose the same mod, click [b]Uninstall[/b]. Everything goes back exactly as it was (your original files are kept in the mod's [i]CPRB_backup[/i] folder until then).

[size=5][b]How to install (Native module zip)[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Open your Warband [i]Modules[/i] folder: Steam > Library > right-click Mount & Blade: Warband > Manage > Browse local files > Modules.
[*]Copy the folder [i]Colored Political Regions & Borders[/i] from the zip into [i]Modules[/i].
[*]Start Warband, choose [i]Colored Political Regions & Borders[/i] in the launcher, click [i]Play[/i] and start a new game.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]When Steam updates a Workshop mod (or you use "Verify integrity of game files"), the patch is removed. Open the patcher and click [b]Install / Update[/b] again.
[*][b]No colours?[/b] Press Space so the game is not paused. In the launcher click [i]Configure > Video[/i] and set [i]Render Method[/i] to [i]DirectX 9[/i].
[*]If the patcher shows [i]PROBLEM: ...[/i], that mod cannot be patched; the message says why.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[b]Tested and stable:[/b] Native and Crusade Against Jihad.
[b]Other mods have not been tested yet.[/b] Please write in the comments how it works in your mod!
[list]
[*]Windows with DirectX 9 (the launcher's default). Mac/Linux (OpenGL) and WSE2 are not supported.
[*]Mods with up to 320 towns, castles and villages and up to 96 kingdoms/factions.
[*]Mods with their own shaders keep their effects (for example waving flags); only the world map part is changed.
[/list]

[center][size=6][b]Türkçe[/b][/size][/center]

Dünya haritasındaki her şehir, kale ve köy bölgesi, onu elinde tutan krallığın rengini alır. Krallıklar arasında koyu bir sınır çizgisi ve iki yanında her krallığın kendi renginde hafif bir hüzme vardır. Uzaklaşınca politik haritayı, yakınlaşınca normal araziyi görürsün; sınırlar her zaman kalır.

[size=5][b]Özellikler[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Uzaklaşınca:[/b] harita krallıkları kendi renkleriyle gösterir (haritadaki şehir isimleriyle aynı renkler).
[*][b]Yakınlaşınca:[/b] renk kaybolur, normal arazi görünür. Sınırlar kalır.
[*][b]Her zaman güncel:[/b] bir kale ya da şehir el değiştirince bölgesi ve köyleri hemen yeni rengini alır.
[*][b]Ctrl + 1:[/b] renklerin ne kadar güçlü olacağını (%0-100) ayarladığın küçük bir panel açar. Çubuğu bırakınca harita gösterir; Done kayıtlı oyununa kaydeder, Esc ya da Cancel geri alır.
[*][b]Ctrl + 2:[/b] kendi krallığının rengini bir renk alanından seç. Fareyi bırakınca toprakların, sınırların ve şehir isimleri değişir; Done kaydeder, Esc ya da Cancel eski rengi geri getirir. Kendi krallığın olmalı (kendine ait bir şehir, kale ya da köy).
[*]Deniz ve nehirler boyanmaz.
[*][b]Native[/b] ile ve [b]CPR&B Map Patcher[/b] programı sayesinde diğer modlarla çalışır.
[/list]
