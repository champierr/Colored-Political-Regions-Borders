MOD NAME:
Colored Political Regions & Borders

SUMMARY (short description field):
A political map for Warband: regions in their owner's colour, fixed borders with a coloured glow, fades to terrain when you zoom in. Native included; patcher for other mods.

CATEGORY: Visuals and Graphics (or Utilities for the patcher)

  Optional file:  cprb_install_tutorial.mp4 (or link the video)

DESCRIPTION (paste everything below):

[center][size=6][b]Colored Political Regions & Borders[/b][/size]
[size=4]A political map for Mount & Blade: Warband[/size][/center]

Every town, castle and village region on the world map takes its owner's colour, and countries are separated by a fixed dark border line with a soft glow of each side's colour.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Zoom out:[/b] the world map becomes a political map in the factions' own colours (the same colours as the map labels).
[*][b]Zoom in:[/b] the colour fades away to the normal terrain; borders stay with their coloured glow.
[*][b]Live:[/b] when a castle or town changes hands, its region and villages change colour right away.
[*][b]Ctrl+1:[/b] a small panel to set the map colour opacity (0-100 %). The setting is saved with your game.
[*]Sea and rivers are left untouched.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[b]Native:[/b] extract [i]Colored_Political_Regions_and_Borders_Native_v1.1.0.zip[/i] into [i]Mount&Blade Warband\Modules\[/i], pick "Colored Political Regions & Borders" in the launcher and start a new game.

[b]Any other mod - CPR&B Map Patcher:[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Download [i]CPRB-Map-Patcher.exe[/i] (here or from [url=https://github.com/champierr/Colored-Political-Regions-Borders/releases/latest]GitHub Releases[/url]).
[*]Run it. The app is not code-signed; if Windows SmartScreen warns, click [i]More info → Run anyway[/i].
[*]Pick your mod (Steam and Workshop mods are listed automatically, or use Browse...).
[*]Choose the starting opacity and press [b]Install / Update[/b].
[*]Start Warband with that mod; a new game is recommended.
[/list]
[b]Uninstall[/b] in the same app removes exactly what was added (originals are backed up in the mod's CPRB_backup folder).
If Steam updates a Workshop mod you patched, run the patcher again.

Mods with their own shaders keep them: only the world map terrain part is replaced, so effects such as waving flags stay.

[size=5][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[b]Tested and stable:[/b] Native and Crusade Against Jihad.
[b]Other mods have not been tested yet[/b] - feedback is very welcome!
[list]
[*]DirectX 9 (the launcher's default). OpenGL (Mac/Linux) and WSE2 are not supported.
[*]Up to 320 towns, castles and villages and up to 96 factions per mod.
[*]Mods that ship their shaders as source code are not supported yet.
[/list]

[center][size=6][b]Türkçe[/b][/size][/center]

Dünya haritasındaki her şehir, kale ve köy bölgesi sahibinin rengini alır; ülkeler arasında sabit koyu bir sınır çizgisi ve iki yanında her ülkenin kendi renginde hafif bir hüzme çizilir.

[size=5][b]Özellikler[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Uzaklaşınca:[/b] dünya haritası, faction'ların kendi renkleriyle (harita yazılarıyla aynı renkler) politik haritaya dönüşür.
[*][b]Yakınlaşınca:[/b] renk kaybolur, fiziki harita görünür; sınırlar renkli hüzmeleriyle kalır.
[*][b]Canlı:[/b] bir kale veya şehir el değiştirince bölgesi ve köyleri hemen yeni rengini alır.
[*][b]Ctrl+1:[/b] harita renk opaklığını (%0-100) ayarladığın küçük bir panel açar. Ayar kayıt dosyanla birlikte saklanır.
[*]Deniz ve nehirler boyanmaz.
[/list]
