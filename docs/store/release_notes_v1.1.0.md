## Colored Political Regions & Borders v1.1.0

A political map for Mount & Blade: Warband: regions in their owner's colour, fixed dark borders with a glow of
each side's colour, and the normal terrain when you zoom in.

### Downloads
- **CPRB-Map-Patcher.exe** – adds CPR&B to any Warband mod (Steam, Workshop or manual install). Uninstall
  removes exactly what it added.
- **Colored_Political_Regions_and_Borders_Native_v1.1.0.zip** – Native with CPR&B; extract into
  `Mount&Blade Warband\Modules\`.
- **cprb_install_tutorial.mp4** – one minute install guide (English and Turkish captions).

Also on Nexus Mods: https://www.nexusmods.com/mbwarband/mods/6853

### Tested
Stable on **Native** and **Crusade Against Jihad**. Other mods have not been tested in game yet – please open an
issue with how it works in yours.

### Notes
- DirectX 9 only (the launcher's default).
- Ctrl+1 on the world map: map colour opacity, saved with your game.
- The exe is not code-signed; Windows SmartScreen may ask once (More info > Run anyway).
- After Steam updates a Workshop mod you patched, run the patcher again.
