## Colored Political Regions & Borders v1.2.0

![Ctrl+2: your kingdom's colour](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/champierr/Colored-Political-Regions-Borders/main/docs/images/gallery_kingdom_colour.jpg)

### New
- **Ctrl+2: your kingdom's colour.** Once you have a kingdom of your own (a town, castle or village you hold for
  yourself), pick its colour from a colour field and a lightness bar in the top right corner. Let go of the
  mouse and your land, borders and town names show it (the window blinks once while the map is drawn);
  **Done** saves it with your game, Esc or **Cancel** brings the old colour back. Without a kingdom of your own
  the panel says so.
- **Ctrl+1 works the same way now:** let go of the opacity bar and the map shows it, **Done** saves it, Esc
  or **Cancel** goes back to the saved value. (Before, the slider saved at once.)
- The panels look like the game's own windows (parchment with a brown frame) instead of a black box.
- **Fewer antivirus false alarms on the exe:** it is now built with a PyInstaller bootloader compiled from
  source instead of the prebuilt one that many other programs share, and it carries product and version
  information.

### Downloads
- **CPRB-Map-Patcher.exe** – adds CPR&B to any Warband mod (Steam, Workshop or manual install). Run it again on
  a mod you patched before to update it. Uninstall removes exactly what it added.
- **Colored_Political_Regions_and_Borders_Native_v1.2.0.zip** – Native with CPR&B; extract into
  `Mount&Blade Warband\Modules\` (replace the old folder).
- **cprb_install_tutorial.mp4** – one minute install guide (English and Turkish captions).

Also on Nexus Mods: https://www.nexusmods.com/mbwarband/mods/6853

### Tested
Stable on **Native** and **Crusade Against Jihad**. The kingdom colour picker was tested on Native. Other mods
have not been tested in game yet – please open an issue with how it works in yours.

### Notes
- DirectX 9 only (the launcher's default).
- The exe is not code-signed; Windows SmartScreen may ask once (More info > Run anyway).
- After Steam updates a Workshop mod you patched, run the patcher again.
