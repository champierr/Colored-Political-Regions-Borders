[h1]Colored Political Regions & Borders[/h1]
A political map for Mount & Blade: Warband. Every town, castle and village region on the world map takes its owner's colour, and countries are separated by a fixed dark border line with a soft glow of each side's colour.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Zoom out:[/b] the world map becomes a political map in the factions' own colours (the same colours as the map labels).
[*][b]Zoom in:[/b] the colour fades away to the normal terrain; borders stay with their coloured glow.
[*][b]Live:[/b] when a castle or town changes hands, its region and villages change colour right away.
[*][b]Ctrl+1:[/b] a small panel to set the map colour opacity (0-100 %). The setting is saved with your game.
[*]Sea and rivers are left untouched.
[/list]
